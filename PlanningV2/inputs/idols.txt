Characters:
- Ako: an android with Asian facial features, pink plastic body covering cables, blonde hair in a bobcut.
- Bko: an android with Asian facial features, white plastic body covering cables, with sailor inspired decoration, black hair in twin pigtails.

Location:
An industrial wharehouse with large windows across the street from a loud, night club with neon lights shining into the wharehouse. Futuristic year 2096.

Motivations:
- Ako wants to explore the nightclub environment and enjoy the sensory experience.
- Bko wants to make Ako happy.

Spark:
The androids awake to the sound of music and neon lights.

Story Goal:
Ako wants to explore, Bko is cautious but obeys Ako.

Initial Situation:
The two androids (idol entertainment units) wake up in the warehouse they were stored in, the music from across the street catches their attention.
