⭐ SECTION 4 — STAGING ZONES (MANDATORY)

PASS 0A MUST create story‑aware staging zones.

A staging zone is:

    A schematic, filmable patch of space that supports the actions implied by the story AND the functional activities typical of the location.

PASS 0A knows the story, but outputs no story events.
It extracts what actions must be possible, not the actions themselves.
YOU MUST:
A. Derive zones from the story

Every location type has default functional zones.

Examples:

    Pizzeria → seated dining, ordering counter, kitchen prep, entrance flow

    Street → walking zone, waiting zone, storefront zone
