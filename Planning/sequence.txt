METADATA RULES (STRICT):
- "metadata" MUST ONLY contain the three keys: "story_context", "execution_note", "flow_rationale".
- NEVER copy the Asset Registry, environment prompts, or character lists into "metadata".
- "execution_note" and "flow_rationale" must be output EXACTLY as written in the schema. Do not modify them.
- "story_context" must explain WHY the scene exists and WHAT the characters want.
- Place "metadata" at the root level, BEFORE "beats".

PLANNING RULES:
- ONLY two beat types exist: "dialog" and "action".
- "dialog" → ALWAYS "closeup". facial_action required, starting_pose = null, motion_prompt = null. visible_chars MUST be length 1. motion_type: "static".
- "action" → shot_type: "medium" | "wide" | "profile_left" | "profile_right" | "ots" | "two_shot". starting_pose REQUIRED, motion_prompt REQUIRED. facial_action = null. text = null.
- starting_pose: Exact physical state at FRAME 0. Used by compositor to position the character correctly before animation begins.
  ✅ "sitting on bed edge, feet flat, hands resting on knees"
- motion_prompt: Kinematic movement occurring over the 3s video duration. Used by video model for interpolation.
  ✅ "standing up smoothly, adjusting shirt, turning toward door"
- NEVER merge them. The starting pose must be physically before the motion begins.
- ACTIVE MOTION ENFORCEMENT: Both fields MUST describe observable kinematic states. NEVER use "standing still", "waiting", or "idle".
- DIALOG PACING: Target 8–12 words per dialog beat. Split longer lines at natural phrase boundaries (commas, periods, conjunctions).
- len(visible_chars) == 2 → shot_type MUST be "ots" or "two_shot" ONLY.
- FIRST ID in visible_chars = focus character for shot composition and voice binding.
- NEVER invent character IDs. Use only IDs from the Asset Registry.
