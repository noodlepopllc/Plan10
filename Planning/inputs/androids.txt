Characters:
- Amy: an android with Asian facial features, red plastic panels covering cables, blonde hair in a bobcut, exposed wiring and physical damage visible.
- Blaire: an android with latinx facial features, blue plastic panels covering cables, long black hair, damage front panel with exposed wiring and missing robootic eye.
- Chad: nerdy guy in the club, wears glasses, suspenders, crew cut and suspenders, red tennis shoes.

Location:
In a futuristic night club with lasers and a large crowd.

Motivations:
- Amy is a pleasure robot and needs to make money in order to recharge.
- Blaire is a pleasure robot and needs to make money for repairs.
- Chad is trying to take advantage of the androids desperation

Spark:
The pleasure androids owner has died, they are performing services in public in order to charge and afford repairs while searching for a new owner.

Story Goal:
The two androids have entered low power mode and worry that if they can't get money for a charge they will shut down permanently.

Initial Situation:
The two androids in the club approach Chad offering services and interview him as possible new owner.
