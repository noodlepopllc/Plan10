Characters:
- Elara: a female elven adventurer, blonde hair, light leather armor, short green tunic, leather boots; confident, talkative, eager for connection.
- Nadia: a young half‑elf barmaid, early 20s, red hair in a ponytail, tattered light blue dress, sandals; polite, reserved, easily overwhelmed by attention.

Location:
A medieval fantasy tavern in the style of the 1400s: a large open room with wooden benches and tables, a bar serving mead, a small kitchen with bread and porridge, and a staircase leading to a few rooms for rent.

Motivations:
- Elara wants friendly conversation and someone to listen to her stories.
- Nadia wants to do her job efficiently and avoid uncomfortable social situations.

Spark:
Nadia brings Elara a beer, and Elara begins enthusiastically telling stories and trying to engage her in extended conversation.

Story Goal:
Elara wants friendly connection, but Nadia becomes increasingly uncomfortable as the conversation drags on.

Initial Situation:
Elara is seated at a wooden table in the tavern. Nadia approaches with a tankard of beer and sets it down in front of her.
