Elara is a female elven adventurer (blonde hair female, light leather armor, short green tunic worn as a dress underneath, leather boots) who is sitting in the fantasy setting tavern. 
Nadia the local bar maid of the tavern (early 20s, Asian half elf, red hair in ponytail, she looks beautiful in a tattered short light blue dress with sandals). 
Nadia brings a beer to Elara. 
Nadia captures Elara's eye and she begins to flirt with the young maiden. Boasting about her latest adventure. 
Nadia politely laughs and smiles beautifully. Nadia in response tells a joke and Elara laughs as if it is the funniest thing she has ever heard. 
It gets awkward and Nadia walks off.
