The air in the subterranean chamber is thick with the smell of damp limestone and stagnant water. A single, flickering torch mounted on a rusted iron bracket casts long, dancing shadows against the weeping stone walls, casting a jaundiced yellow light over the uneven floor. The ground is composed of cracked flagstones slick with a thin film of moisture. To the left, heavy oak doors reinforced with iron bands are bolted shut; to the right, a deep, jagged fissure in the masonry reveals a void of absolute darkness.

Alora stands center-stage, her small frame trembling slightly. She wears a tattered, low-cut brown dress made of rough linen that clings to her skin, its hem frayed and stained with mud. Her blonde hair falls in tangled waves over her shoulders, and her face is smudged with soot across the cheekbone. Her hands are bound before her waist by heavy hempen rope, and she stands with her weight shifted back, her gaze fixed on the floor.

Quin stands five paces away, her posture rigid and regal. She wears a gown of deep crimson silk that flows to the floor in heavy folds, contrasting sharply with the grime of the cell. Her black hair is pulled into a severe, tight bun, held in place by a jagged gold crown set with obsidian shards. She holds a staff of polished dark wood in her right hand, her grip firm as she faces Alora, her chin tilted upward.

***

Quin takes a step forward, the silk of her skirts rusting against the stone. "The truth is a heavy burden, Alora. I did not seek this path for pleasure."

Alora lifts her head, her eyes narrowing. She pulls against the ropes around her waist, the hemp biting into her skin. "You took me from my chambers. You put me in this hole."

Quin stops, her expression hardening. She raises her staff slightly. "I took you to protect you from a throne built on lies."

Alora lunges forward as far as the ropes allow, her face contorting. "My mother is the Queen! You are the usurper!"

Quin closes her eyes for a moment before exhaling slowly. She paces a small circle in front of Alora, her movements deliberate and graceful. "Your mother is a weaver of shadows, niece. She traded our lineage's purity for the dark arts to maintain her grip on the throne."

Alora laughs, a harsh, jagged sound that echoes off the damp walls. She spits toward Quin’s feet. "A fairy tale to justify your greed."

Quin stops abruptly and points the tip of her staff at Alora’s chest. A faint, violet hum emanles from the wood. "The crown was stolen from our bloodline generations ago. I only took up the arts to counter her influence—to shield what remains of our house."

Alora ignores the warning, stepping toward the sorceress despite the magical hum. She reaches out with her bound hands, grasping at the air between them. "Then let me go! If you are my protector, set me free!"

Quin recoils slightly, her face twisting into a mask of frustration. She strikes the floor with the base of her staff, and a pulse of energy ripples through the air, knocking Alora backward onto her haunches. 

Alora gasps as she hits the ground, her hands hitting the wet stone. She scrambles to sit up, her hair falling over her face. "You're just afraid of her power."

Quin moves toward her, kneeling in the dirt until they are eye-to-eye. The crown on Quin’s head glints in the torchlight. "I am afraid of what she will do to you if you remain her puppet. I am trying to save your soul from her rot."

Alora lunges forward again, this time grabbing the hem of Quin's red dress. She pulls with all her might, her face inches from her aunt’s. "You are a monster in a pretty dress. You want my throne because you think you can do it better."

Quin grabs Alora’s wrists, pinning them against the floor. The physical contact causes both women to freeze. 

"I want you to be safe," Quin hisses, her voice cracking with a rare note of desperation. "The crown is mine by right, yes, but I would burn it to ash before I let her turn you into a husk."

Alora stares at her, the silence of the dungeon pressing in on them. She sees the tremor in Quin’s hands. 

Alora pulls back, her face hardening into a mask of cold defiance. "Then you have already lost me."

Quin stands slowly, releasing Alora's wrists. She turns her back and looks toward the dark fissure in the wall. 

Alora stands as well, pulling herself up with effort. She ignores the pain in her arms and stares at the heavy iron-bound door behind them. "Then let me go to my mother."

Quin turns back, her face a mask of stone once more. She raises her staff high above her head. 

Alora screams as a wave of kinetic force slams into her chest, throwing her backward against the stone wall. 

The impact knocks the breath from Alora's lungs. She slumps against the masonry, gasping for air.

Quin stands in the center of the room, the staff glowing with a steady, pulsing light. "You will stay here until you choose to listen."
