The entire story takes place within the living room of a small 3 bedroom house in the suburbs. A married couple (Melissa light skinned eastern european female late 20s blonde hair in pony tail, she is slim, wearing tank top and short, George male early 40s wearing flannel pajamas, medium build).
George is reading his favorite vertigo comic. He comments on how wonderful and inventive vertigo is and how they saved the comic industry with books like "sandman" the only comic to win a Hugo.  
His wife an independent comic publisher and part owner of their boutique comic book store gets really annoyed with him. She feels vertigo was just DC cashing in on the comic book industry with the recent popularity of lesser known comic publishers. She got into comics with image comics and feels what Todd Mcfarlane did when he left Marvel was truly what saved the entire comic book industry. She talks about these topics as George listens patiently. He makes a few counter points about how mature Batman was under Vertigo with his wife scoffing about how commercial Batman has become and it definitely does not belong under DCs so called indie label. The argument ends with the husband smiling and telling her discussions like this is why he is the luckiest man in the world. She laughs at this and smiles happily.
