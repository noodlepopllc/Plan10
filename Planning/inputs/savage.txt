THEME/STYLE:
inspired by 1950s sci-fi, barbarella, buck rogers, panavision 60

Characters:
- Sora: Space explorer, early 20s aged female, asian features, silver bikini with matching gloves and boots.
- Lindsy: Late 20s aged, Eastern european female with red hair, former slave, wearing chain mail bikini, metal choker with broken chain attached,  and carries a red laser sword.

Location:
On a savage desert alien world.

Motivations:
- Sora is trying to get back to her crashed ship to see if the communications still work so she can call home.
- Lindsy is an escaped slave trying to avoid her former captors and is helping Sora in order to escape this planet. 

Spark:
Sora emerges from her escape pod where she meets with Lindsy.

Story Goal:
The two women want to survive the world long enough to find the spaceship to get off the planet.

Initial Situation:
The two women are at an oasis surrounded by strange hostile looking beasts.
