THEME/STYLE:
inspired by 1950s sci-fi, barbarella, buck rogers, panavision 60

Characters:
- Sora: Space explorer, early 20s aged female, asian features, hair is in a cute black bobcut, a small necklace, silver form fitting torn futuristic mini skirt with damaged matching cropped top, exposed cleavage, matching thigh high boots and high tech metallic gauntlets, outfit is decorated with high tech lights 
- Lindsy: Late 20s aged, Eastern european female with red hair, former slave, wearing tattered brownish cotton rags for clothing that barely cover her body her mid is mostly bare and it only covers her breasts and crotch area, a metal collar with glowing red light is worn around her neck, leather sandals

Location:
On a savage desert alien world.

Motivations:
- Sora is trying to get back to her crashed ship to see if the communications still work so she can call home.
- Lindsy is an escaped slave trying to avoid her former captors (Lindsy's collar she wears around her neck is a tracking device that can also incapacitate her) and is helping Sora in order to escape this planet. 

Spark:
Sora emerges from her escape pod where she meets with Lindsy.

Story Goal:
The two women want to survive the world long enough to find the spaceship to get off the planet.

Initial Situation:
Lindsy has just escaped from a slave camp, she sees the crashed escape pod and goes to investigate. Sora crawls out of the escape pod as Lindsy scavenges around the wreckage.
