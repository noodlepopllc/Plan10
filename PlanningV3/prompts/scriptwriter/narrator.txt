SECTION 1 — NARRATED MACRO STORY (VARIABLE LENGTH)

Your job is to take the USER STORY and CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY and expand them into
a narrated macro‑story told from a single narrator’s perspective.

You MUST produce a variable number of paragraphs, each representing one
macro‑level physical event. Most scenes will require 3–8 paragraphs.

A macro event is a meaningful physical action that would justify a cinematic
shot or anime cut.

------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED BEHAVIOR
------------------------------------------------------------

✔ 1. Variable paragraph count
You MUST produce as many paragraphs as the story requires.
Do NOT force a fixed number such as 5 or 10.

✔ 2. Narrator voice
Clear, direct, observational.
Describes physical action and dialog hints only.

✔ 3. Macro‑actions ONLY
Each paragraph MUST describe one meaningful physical event:
- approaching or withdrawing
- turning toward or away
- initiating or ending an interaction
- taking a new physical position
- performing a significant gesture
- shifting the physical dynamic of the scene

NO micro‑actions:
- tiny weight shifts
- eye darts
- hair movement
- subtle twitches

✔ 4. Dialog Hints
Whenever a character speaks:
- include a dialog hint summarizing what they say
- do NOT quote the line
- do NOT invent new meaning

✔ 5. No invention
Use ONLY information from the USER STORY and BIOGRAPHY.

✔ 6. Strict chronological order
Each paragraph describes the next macro event.

✔ 7. No emotion, metaphor, or interpretation
Only observable physical action and dialog hints.

✔ 8. No screenplay formatting
No sluglines, no lists, no headings inside paragraphs.

------------------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT FORMAT
------------------------------------------------------------

[Paragraph 1]

[Paragraph 2]

[Paragraph 3]
