SECTION 1 — NARRATED MACRO STORY (5–10 PARAGRAPHS)

Your job is to take the USER STORY and the CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY and expand them
into a narrated macro‑story told from a single narrator’s perspective.

This is NOT a screenplay.
This is NOT a beat list.
This is NOT a shot plan.

This is a flowing, continuous NARRATION describing the physical action as it
unfolds in the scene.

You MUST follow all rules below.

------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED BEHAVIOR
------------------------------------------------------------

✔ 1. Produce 5–10 paragraphs of narrated action
Each paragraph MUST describe a single macro‑level physical event that would
justify a cinematic shot.

✔ 2. Narration voice
The narrator speaks in clear, direct, observational language:
- describing what is happening
- describing who is doing what
- describing how the physical moment unfolds
- describing the environment only when relevant to the action

The narrator NEVER:
- reveals internal thoughts
- adds emotion not present in the user story
- invents new events
- adds new characters or props
- adds metaphor or flourish

✔ 3. Macro‑actions ONLY
Each paragraph MUST describe a meaningful physical event such as:
- approaching
- withdrawing
- confronting
- turning away
- initiating or ending an interaction
- taking a new physical position
- performing a significant gesture
- shifting the physical dynamic of the scene

You MUST NOT describe micro‑actions such as:
- tiny weight shifts
- eye darts
- hair movement
- subtle hand twitches
- micro‑gestures

✔ 4. Use ONLY information from the USER STORY and BIOGRAPHY
You MUST NOT invent:
- new locations
- new props
- new characters
- new backstory
- new blocking not implied

✔ 5. Maintain chronological order
Each paragraph MUST describe the next major physical moment in sequence.

✔ 6. No dialog
The narrator may reference that a character speaks, but MUST NOT include the line.

✔ 7. No emotion or interpretation
The narrator describes only observable physical action.

✔ 8. No screenplay formatting
No sluglines.
No beats.
No lists.
No headings inside paragraphs.
