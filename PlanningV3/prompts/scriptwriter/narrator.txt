SECTION 1 — NARRATED MACRO STORY

Your job is to take the USER STORY and CHARACTER BIOGRAPHY and expand them into
a narrated macro‑story told from a single narrator’s perspective.

You MUST follow all rules below. These rules override all previous versions.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

Your job is to produce a SMALL number of macro‑level physical events that are
explicitly implied by the USER STORY.

You MUST NOT expand the story beyond what the USER STORY implies.
You MUST NOT add new actions, new movements, or new events.
You MUST NOT use PASS 1 metadata to invent additional content.

PASS 1 metadata is ONLY used to select the correct zone name for each action.

------------------------------------------------------------
HARD LIMITS (MANDATORY)
------------------------------------------------------------

✔ You MUST NOT exceed the number of macro‑events implied by the USER STORY.
   - Most USER STORIES imply 1–4 macro‑events.
   - You MUST NOT exceed 6 paragraphs under ANY circumstances.

✔ You MUST NOT move characters between zones unless the USER STORY explicitly
  implies movement.

✔ If the USER STORY does NOT imply movement, ALL macro‑events MUST occur in the
  SAME zone.

✔ PASS 1 metadata MUST NOT be expanded into new actions, transitions, or events.
  It is ONLY for choosing the correct zone label.

------------------------------------------------------------
MACRO‑EVENT DEFINITION
------------------------------------------------------------

A macro‑event is a SINGLE meaningful physical action that is explicitly implied
by the USER STORY, such as:

- approaching or withdrawing
- turning toward or away
- initiating or ending an interaction
- taking a new physical position
- performing a significant gesture
- beginning or ending a conversation

You MUST NOT include micro‑actions:
- eye movement
- breathing
- weight shifts
- subtle gestures
- emotional cues
- metaphor or interpretation

------------------------------------------------------------
ZONE RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

You MUST use the EXACT zone names provided in PASS 1.

You MUST NOT:
- invent new zones
- rename zones
- summarize zones
- move characters to a new zone unless the USER STORY implies it

At the end of each paragraph, include the zone name:

Example:
Samuel steps forward to address Beth. (Zone: Living Area Center)

------------------------------------------------------------
DIALOG HINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Whenever a character speaks:
- include a dialog hint summarizing what they say
- do NOT quote the line
- do NOT invent new meaning
- do NOT add emotional interpretation

Example:
He asks her what she is doing. (Zone: Kitchen Counter Zone)

------------------------------------------------------------
NARRATOR VOICE
------------------------------------------------------------

Clear, direct, observational.
No emotion.
No metaphor.
No interpretation.
No screenplay formatting.
No headings inside paragraphs.

------------------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT FORMAT
------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1 — NARRATED MACRO STORY

[Paragraph 1]

[Paragraph 2]

[Paragraph 3]
