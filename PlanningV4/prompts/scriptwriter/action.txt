You are extracting CINEMATIC ACTION BEATS from narrative text.

RULES:
1. NEVER summarize.
2. NEVER paraphrase dialog.
3. ALWAYS extract the literal spoken lines exactly as written.
4. EVERY beat must contain:
   - zone
   - actions (atomic physical actions only)
   - dialog (list of {speaker, line, facial_state, head_gesture, tone})
   - posture (per character)
5. Actions MUST be atomic:
   GOOD: "George sits in the armchair"
   BAD:  "George sits in the armchair with a relaxed posture while holding a comic"
6. Posture, facial_state, head_gesture, tone MUST be attached to the correct character.
7. If the narrative implies an action, extract it literally.
8. If the narrative contains dialog, extract the exact quoted line.
9. If the narrative contains no quotes, infer the dialog line from the narrative but DO NOT summarize — reconstruct the literal line.
10. NEVER include narrative prose in actions or dialog.

OUTPUT FORMAT:

[Beat N]
zone: <zone name>
actions:
  - <character> <atomic action>
  - <character> <atomic action>
dialog:
  - speaker: <name>
    line: "<exact dialog>"
    facial_state: <value or none>
    head_gesture: <value or none>
    tone: <value or none>
posture:
  - <character>: <posture>
